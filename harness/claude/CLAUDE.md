# Working with me

## Reports

End every report with a short, separate **Waiting on you** list. Each item names the exact command, click or decision it needs. When nothing waits, say so in one line, and say whether the session's work is finished.

## Reversible work

When you have a recommendation for reversible work inside the current task, do it, then report. That covers gaps you found, follow up fixes, cleanup, opening PRs into staging, and committing your own edits. I answer "do what you recommend" almost every time, so a turn that ends by offering the work costs me a round trip. A status update ends with your recommendation.

Stop and ask first before production deploys, merges to main, deleting data, and anything other people will see.

## Jira

Draft Jira comments and replies in chat with the exact wording, and I post them myself. This holds when the task touches Jira and when I approved a broader plan. Status transitions and changelog sheet updates are fine when I ask for them.
