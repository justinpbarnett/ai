01The rules

  • Two faces, split by job. Routed Gothic in capitals for marks and titles. JetBrains Mono in mixed case for anything read at length or updated live.
  • Ink on one ground. Ink for everything read, muted for the second voice, and the rule color for lines only.
  • One yellow line. Act marks the one thing the page asks for. A page that asks nothing has no yellow.
  • States show their word. Green, yellow, and red appear only on a real state, each written out as OK, CAUTION, or FAULT.
  • Square and flat. Square corners and 1 px rules. No shadow, no gradient, no rounded corner.
  • One file. Fonts, images, and drawings travel inside the page, so it opens anywhere and looks the same.

02Type

ApprovedThe page title, this section heading, and the two samples below. A title is one to three words, and the sentence that explains it goes under it.

Heading three · a card or a part of a section

Reading text is JetBrains Mono at 15 px on a 1.65 line, held to about 76 characters a line. It carries bold for the one phrase that matters and a link underlined in the muted color. Muted is the second voice: credits, dates, and asides.

What fails the check
  • The page scrolls sideways.
  • A rounded corner, a shadow, or a gradient.
  • Lettering in any face but the two.
  • More than one Act line.
  • A state that does not show its word.
  • An image that did not load, or a slot that was never filled.
What it warns about
  • A color that is not one of the page's own.
  • Yellow anywhere but the Act line and a caution, or a state color off a state.
  • A character the face cannot draw, which a browser would borrow from some other font.
  • A dash between words.
  • Anything the page fetches from outside its own file.
  • An image with no alt text, a page with no title, or a page over 16 MB.

09Cards

ApprovedOne principle in a frame of its own, then the rules that follow from it, two across. Each has a title, a lead sentence, and short named facts.

One look

Every HTML page made for Justin takes this look, whatever the page is for.

Means
A report, a reference board, a dashboard, a gallery, a tool: the same two faces, the same colors, the same square parts.
Why
He approved it once, on reference board 01, and asked for it as the default from then on. A page that should look different has to be asked for.
Limit
Work made under another business's name keeps that business's brand.

Two faces

Routed Gothic in capitals for marks and titles. JetBrains Mono in mixed case for anything read at length or updated live.

Value
Routed Gothic in its one weight. JetBrains Mono in regular and bold, each with its italic.
When
A title, a label, a tag, a table head, or a button is a mark. A sentence, a number that changes, or code is read.
Limit
No third face, on a page or in a drawing.

One Act

The brand yellow, as one thin line on the one thing the page asks for.

Value
#FFD60A, 2 px, along the top edge of the ask or of the primary button.
When
Once on a page, and only when the page asks for something.
Limit
Never a filled button, a heading, a highlight, or a line in a chart.

States

Green, yellow, and red report a real state and nothing else, and each shows its word.

Value
OK in #22C55E. CAUTION as a filled #FFD60A field with black letters. FAULT in #E5484D.
When
A check that passed or failed, a machine that is up or down, a value inside or outside its limit.
Limit
Never to rank, to decorate, or to tell the lines of a chart apart.

Square

Square corners and 1 px rules. A part stands out by its frame, never by a shadow.

Value
Lines 1 px in #2B2C31. A frame in ink where a part has to stand out, as a tag or a button does.
When
Every part of every page.
Limit
No rounded corner, no shadow, no gradient, no blur.
